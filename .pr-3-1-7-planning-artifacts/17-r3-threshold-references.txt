THRESHOLD-VALIDITY READING (Thread 2 commentary):
  R3 = 0.0833 was set to the empirically observed value, not derived
  from a quality target. Triple-invariance across three radically
  different upstream interventions (bulk-reembed, α backfill) confirms
  the threshold is currently bounded BELOW by Stage 1 universe size,
  not by Stage 2/3 quality. As a release gate, 0.0833 is a no-op floor
  — every shippable v2 build that doesn't actively regress Stage 1
  candidate-set arithmetic will hit it.

  Implication for Thread 3: R3=0.0833 does not BLOCK option (a) "ship
  the flip". Whether the flip should ship at this aggregateScore is a
  product question (do we accept ~1/12 fixture pass-rate in production?)
  — not a release-gate question.
