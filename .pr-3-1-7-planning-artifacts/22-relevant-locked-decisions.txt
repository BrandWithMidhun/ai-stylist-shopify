SUMMARY OF LOCKED DECISIONS RELEVANT TO 3.1.7
================================================================

- The flip is a single-seam edit in registry.server.ts (D6 mech.6).
- Variant-loading should follow D7 mech.6 pattern: in v2 tool stub,
  not in Stage 6, to keep the orchestrator pure-compute.
- ProductCard wire format is locked to mirror legacy (D9 mech.5);
  variantId/available placeholders are an implementation gap to close,
  not a contract redesign.
- Variant-availability filter is structural and lives in Stage 1
  EXISTS predicate; OOS-substitute Stage 5 path was deliberately
  deferred (mech.5 D6).
- APPROVED-only filter on ProductTag is a project-wide trust-gate
  posture; relaxing it would be a project-level redecision, not a
  3.1.7-scope change.
